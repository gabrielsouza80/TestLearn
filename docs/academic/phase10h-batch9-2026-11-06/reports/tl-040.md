## Solução independente / Independent pre-key solution

Underestimating maintenance effort for automated scripts is a risk, unlike reduced repetitive work, greater repeatability and useful automated metrics, which are potential benefits. Maintenance effort itself is an expected cost; underestimating it creates planning/budget/sustainability risk. Benefits are not guaranteed, but alternative unforeseen costs do not reverse the requested risk-versus-benefit polarity.
