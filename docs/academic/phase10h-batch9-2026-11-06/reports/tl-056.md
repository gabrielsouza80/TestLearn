## Solução independente / Independent pre-key solution

Weekly feedback from intended warehouse users during workflow evolution can reveal misunderstood needs early and reduce rework risk. It neither makes later requirements changes unnecessary nor replaces acceptance criteria nor proves every staff member will accept final implementation. Weekly is this example’s frequency, not a universally required cadence. Picking versus broader preparação de encomendas has lexical scope difference but does not change the feedback principle.
