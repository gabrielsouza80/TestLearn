## Solução independente / Independent pre-key solution

An operational service migrating database engine changes its environment and can affect data/behaviour even with unchanged application code. Maintenance testing considers migration impacts; prior results do not automatically prove compatibility, maintenance is not code-only, and data checks do not substitute all behavioural regression. Can/pode describes potential impact, not guaranteed failure or a universal fixed test obligation.
