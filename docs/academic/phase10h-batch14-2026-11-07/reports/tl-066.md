A weekly management summary may suit the steering group, but using it as the sole blocker notification delays the rapid operational visibility explicitly required by the distributed team.

A daily live call can supplement communication, but as the sole record without retained status it is unsuitable for asynchronous access across time zones and for weekly management synthesis.
